Business Model Narrative

VNC Nutrition Foods Private Limited is a Gujarat-based agri-food processing enterprise specialising in the procurement, processing, and wholesale distribution of groundnut-based products. The company's primary product is HPS (Hand Picked Selected) Groundnuts (HSN 12021019), which constitutes approximately 75-82% of its total taxable sales value. The company operates a manufacturing facility at Survey No. 181, Sarsav, Kadi, Mehsana — a strategically located agri-processing hub in North Gujarat — with installed capacity for groundnut cleaning, grading, and processing.

The business model is predominantly B2B wholesale, with 96-100% of sales to GST-registered entities. The company sources raw groundnuts primarily from Gujarat (94%+ of purchases in FY26) and Rajasthan (5%), processes them at its Kadi facility, and distributes to food manufacturers, traders, and distributors across Gujarat (90%), Telangana (6%), Maharashtra (1.2%), and Uttarakhand (1.1%). The company is also expanding into value-added products including Peanut Butter manufacturing, with a new plant under commissioning (Yes Bank condition: RM visit report by 30 Sep 2026).

The promoter family (Chosaliya family) holds 100% of the equity, with Vinodbhai Nanjibhai Patel and Harshadbhai Vinodbhai Chosaliya as the two directors. The company has a working capital-intensive business model with significant inventory holding (groundnut procurement is seasonal) and extended credit to distributors. Banking facilities are primarily from Yes Bank (CC Rs. 5.50 Cr + DLOD facilities + Term Loans), with the entire banking relationship consolidated at Yes Bank as per the facility letter condition requiring 100% routing.

HIGH PRIORITY — 3 Months

4. Customer Diversification Strategy

Cash Conversion Cycle of 71.83 days is too long for a commodity trading business. Target: Reduce inventory days from 63 to 50 (implement just-in-time procurement for non-seasonal stock), maintain debtor days below 25 (enforce credit terms), and extend payable days to 30-35 days. 22 circular/cyclic GST entities identified. Yes Bank restricts certain group company debtors from DP calculation.
